How to Play
The Simplest Game Possible is a two-player game of Xs and Os. Build the sequence carefully and try to complete your pattern before your opponent does.

1. Take Your Turn

Player 1 goes first.
On each turn, choose either X or O.
Your chosen letter is added to the shared sequence.
Players take turns until someone wins or the sequence reaches 30 letters.

2. Your Goal

Player 1
Create 2 identical, non-overlapping sequences of 5 consecutive letters.

Player 2
Create 4 identical, non-overlapping sequences of 3 consecutive letters.

Any combination of X and O can form a sequence. You don't choose a pattern beforehand.

3. Win the Game

The moment a player completes their objective, the game ends.

Player 1 completes their 2 matching 5-letter sequences → Player 1 wins.
Player 2 completes their 4 matching 3-letter sequences → Player 2 wins.
Both complete their objectives on the same move → Draw.
30 letters are reached without a winner → Draw.

Choose wisely. Every letter counts.
